# 🛍️ Customer Behavior Analysis

**Overview**
End-to-end data analysis project to understand customer purchase behavior and identify key revenue drivers using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

---

**Dataset**
- ~3,900 customer purchase records  
- Includes demographics, transaction details, and behavioral data  
- Features: Age, Gender, Category, Purchase Amount, Rating, Subscription, Shipping, etc.

---

**Tools Used**
- Python (Pandas, NumPy) – Data cleaning & feature engineering  
- SQL – Business analysis & insights  
- Power BI – Dashboard & visualization  

---

**Dashboard**
Interactive dashboard tracking:
- KPIs: AOV, Customer Count, Avg Rating  
- Revenue by Category & Age Group  
- Customer Segmentation & Trends  
- Filters: Gender, Category, Subscription, Shipping  


---

**Key Results**
- Revenue concentrated in male customers (~68%) and clothing category (~45%)  
- Average spend (~₹59–60) consistent across segments  
- Discounts increase volume, not order value  
- High-rated products are underutilized  
- Revenue is stable across seasons
